# EAC revision notes before Study 2

This manuscript revision deliberately does **not** add another broad theoretical claim. It narrows and strengthens the submission around validated endogenous adaptation.

Changes made:

- Retitled the paper to foreground the technical question rather than the anthropomorphic “second dimension of smartness” framing.
- Replaced “preregistered” with **prespecified** for Study 1; the protocol was frozen before execution but was not claimed to have been publicly preregistered.
- Reworked the novelty section to explicitly distinguish EAC from experience replay, STaR, Quiet-STaR, Memory-of-Thought, LoRA, verifier-filtered self-improvement, sharpening, and recursive self-training.
- Added the critical distinction between **no new environmental observation/corrective label** and **no new information**. A pass/fail verifier contributes evaluative information.
- Retained all Study 1 results and caveats. H5/H8 remain mechanism-isolation tests; H9-H11 remain limited by privileged latent validation.
- Added a frozen Study 2 protocol using a real pretrained causal LM, transformer LoRA, disjoint candidate/evaluation sets, pass/fail-only verification, and matched replay/self-training controls.
- Prespecified six Study 2 primary hypotheses and Holm correction before results are observed.
- Rewrote the limitations and conclusion so transformer-scale EAC is explicitly **pending**, not already validated.

Do not add Study 2 result language until the confirmatory ZIP has been returned and independently audited.
